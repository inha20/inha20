Biography & Philosophy

About the Research

I am an independent researcher and a graduate of Inha Technical College, conducting a long-term research program on Structure Recognition — the study of how humans discover structure, assign meaning to it, and generate new research questions from what they observe.

My research began with a simple empirical observation: certain patterns in 4-variable Karnaugh maps — like the checkerboard formed by XOR/XNOR functions — feel visually distinct from other Boolean function patterns. That intuition evolved into a series of interconnected investigations into structural invariance, topological equivalence under variable permutations, and the cognitive mechanisms underlying mathematical insight.

A Starting Insight & Active Direction
The 4-variable Boolean function space contains exactly 216 = 65,536 distinct functions. Under axis-swap symmetry — where F(A,B,C,D) = F(C,D,A,B) — these functions are not all structurally independent. Some are symmetric under the swap, some form pairs, and others form larger orbits.

Research Question: When the 65,536 functions are classified by axis-swap symmetry and gate generators, how many distinct equivalence classes result? This question bridges group theory and combinatorics, forming the foundation of Branch 8 (Boolean Function Space Theory).

Research Philosophy: Humans discover structure through visual and spatial intuition; AI expands the explanation space. The collaboration between human structural insight and AI's capacity for systematic combinatorial exploration is itself an object of rigorous study — not merely a convenience.

Historical Progression

Concept Genealogy

The sequence below records the actual developmental path of the research program — tracing discovery from intuition to formalization.

1. Pattern (Karnaugh Map Checkerboard)
First empirical observation: Alternating visual checkerboard patterns for XOR/XNOR logic functions.
2. Layer Structure (Hamming Weights)
Discovery: Hamming-weight layers mathematically govern the locations of symmetric function patterns.
3. Equivalence & Structural Invariance
Observation: Variable rearrangements preserve invariant structural topologies across map re-orderings.
4. Structure Recognition Theory (SRT)
Meta-question: Why do certain structures trigger research inquiry while others remain unnoticed?
5. Human-AI Collaboration Model (HARCT)
Division of cognitive labor: Humans discover structure intuitively; AI systematically expands the explanation space.
6. Boolean Function Space Theory (Branch 8)
Current mathematical frontier: Gate-generated sub-spaces and Post's lattice as rigorous foundations.
